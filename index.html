Smart CDL

Study cards, practice quizzes, and full-length exams for the commercial driver license knowledge tests — built for your state, working offline with bundled or downloaded content.

Study that works

  • Study cards that condense your state's requirements down to what you actually need to know
  • Short, focused quizzes for every topic — a few minutes at a time
  • Full-length practice exams scored at the real 80% passing mark
  • General Knowledge, Air Brakes, Combination Vehicles, and every endorsement

Learn from every mistake

  • Wrong answers are explained on the spot, while the question is still fresh
  • After each exam, step back through every question — your answer beside the correct one

Works offline

  • The whole app runs on your device — study in the cab, at the dock, anywhere without internet
  • Download fresh study content when online, then keep studying offline

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads
  • No ads or analytics — your answers and study progress stay on your device
  • Your progress stays on your device, under your control
