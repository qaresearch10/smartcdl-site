How we build

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No ads, no tracking. Our apps carry no advertising, no analytics, and no attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your data stays on your device. What you enter and the progress you make is stored in your device's private app storage. We run no servers that receive it.
  • Works offline. Downloading reference material aside, everything runs with no connection.
  • Pay once. One-time purchases, never subscriptions, and they restore free of charge on any device signed in to the same store account.
