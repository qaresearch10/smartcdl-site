Privacy Policy

Smart CDL · Effective 1 August 2026

Smart CDL has no accounts. Your name, answers, saved quiz and exam reviews, and detailed study progress stay on your device. Content updates and optional notifications use Google cloud services. Optional usage analytics is off by default; if you enable Share usage data, we learn which licensing states and study features are used. The information exchanged is described below.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Smart CDL mobile application ("the app"), published by SmartApps Studio. Questions: smart.apps.ca@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your device's private app storage:

  • The name you enter during setup
  • Your selected state, CDL class, and endorsement selections
  • Study progress, answers, saved reviews, scores, and paused quizzes or exams
  • Which in-app purchases you own and your app settings
  • Downloaded study content, content-version information, and notification and usage-sharing preferences

Your name, answers, reviews, scores, progress, CDL class, and endorsements are not uploaded to our content service. The selected state is sent to request the correct state content and, if enabled, to subscribe to that state's update notifications. If you enable usage sharing, the selected licensing state is also included in usage events. Study databases and saved question records remain encrypted on your device; other preferences are stored in private app storage.

3. How we use technical information

Google Cloud and Firebase process the technical information needed to deliver content, protect decryption-key access, and send notifications. Service request logs may contain IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, response status, and other standard network details.

Usage sharing uses Firebase Analytics and Google Analytics to understand which licensing states users select and whether they use handbook downloads and reading, study cards, quizzes, and exams. It is optional and off by default. If enabled, the app sends the selected licensing state, feature-use events, download outcomes, and whether an attempt is a quiz or exam, new, resumed, retaken, or reviewed. These events do not contain your name, email, questions, answers, scores, detailed study history, encryption keys, tokens, or download URLs. Firebase also processes an Analytics installation identifier, app and device information, and automatic SDK events such as session and lifecycle activity. While sharing is enabled, the SDK can automatically report store purchases with product identifiers or names, purchase amount, quantity and currency. It does not receive your payment card, billing address or store account details. Network information can provide an approximate region; the selected licensing state is a separate preference and does not establish where you live.

Reports use pseudonymous installation identifiers and are not fully anonymous. The app has no analytics account ID and does not join your activity to your entered name or across your devices. We do not sell personal information, run ads, build advertising profiles, or link usage reports to advertising identities. Content requests are used for delivery and security, not as a substitute for optional usage analytics.

4. When the app connects to the internet

Bundled and previously downloaded study content works offline. The app connects for these features:

  • Content updates. At startup, when returning to the app, or when you choose Check for content update, the app may contact our Google Cloud content service. It sends your selected state, app build number, and requested content release and file identifiers. Firebase App Check sends app/device attestation and installation information to verify that requests come from an approved app. Verified, encrypted study databases and artwork download from Google Cloud Storage over HTTPS. Decryption keys are delivered securely and stored using the device's secure key storage. Your study records are not included in these requests.
  • Optional notifications. If you enable content update notifications in Settings and allow the system permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes an installation/registration identifier and subscriptions for general content and your selected state. On iOS, Apple Push Notification service also processes a device push token. Notifications announce available content; they do not contain your answers, progress, or decryption keys. You can turn them off in Settings or the device's notification settings.
  • Optional usage analytics. If you enable Settings → Share usage data, Firebase Analytics sends usage events and SDK information to Google for the reports described above. You can turn sharing off at any time. It is independent of content updates, purchases and notification permission.
  • State CDL handbooks. When you download a handbook, your device requests the PDF directly from the state agency's official publishing website. That publisher receives standard request information such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Purchases. Google Play on Android and Apple's App Store on iOS process purchases and restore ownership. We do not receive your payment card, billing address, or store account details.
  • Contacting support. If you choose to email support, your email app sends the message you review and submit, including your email address and any information you choose to include.

Fonts are bundled in the app and do not download at runtime. Google's handling of information is described in its privacy policy and Firebase privacy information; Apple's is described in its privacy policy.

5. Purchases

Smart CDL offers one-time purchases to unlock study content. They are not subscriptions and do not renew. The platform store handles payment, receipts, and refunds. Purchases are tied to your store account and can be restored on another device using the same platform store account through Settings → Restore purchases.

6. Permissions

  • Internet access — for content updates, notifications, optional usage analytics, handbook downloads, and store services
  • Billing — for in-app purchases on Android
  • Notifications — requested only when you enable content update notifications

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos, device location, or files outside its own storage.

7. Children

Smart CDL is a study aid for commercial driver license tests, which require applicants to be at least 18 years old. It is not directed to children.

8. Deleting your information

  • Settings → Reset progress clears locally saved study history.
  • Turning off content notifications removes the app's topic subscriptions and messaging registration when the device can reach Firebase. Offline cleanup is retried after reconnection.
  • Turning off Share usage data stops new Analytics collection and resets the local Analytics identifier and unsent Analytics buffers. It does not delete information already received by Google. Resetting study progress does not delete server-side Analytics events.
  • Removing the app clears its private app data. Device backups, secure key storage, and store purchase records follow the platform's own rules.

We have no account or server copy of your saved study records to delete. Technical service logs, identifiers, and previously collected Analytics data follow the configured service retention settings and providers' retention rules. For questions about technical information, Analytics data, or a message you sent to support, contact us at the address below.
